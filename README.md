# YT-Blinders: Learn on YouTube. Don't Get Lost in It.

## Inspiration
YouTube is a fantastic learning resource, but its carefully designed, clickbait thumbnails are incredibly good at grabbing attention. You might search for a tutorial, but those thumbnails easily distract us, leading down completely unrelated rabbit holes. 

## Why we built it
While there are existing extensions to hide thumbnails, they weren't open-source or didn't work exactly as desired. YT-Blinders was built to solve this specific problem and serve as a first Chrome extension project.

## What it does
YT-Blinders is a Chrome extension for people who use YouTube to learn, research, or follow tutorials but often get distracted. It helps you stay focused by:

- **Hiding thumbnails**: Keeps the video cards and titles, but removes the visual temptation.
- **Hiding entire cards**: Removes the video completely, including its thumbnail and text.

It works seamlessly across YouTube's:
- Home page
- Search results
- Related videos
- Comments

The goal isn't to stop using YouTube. It's to make YouTube useful without constantly tempting you with what to watch next.
